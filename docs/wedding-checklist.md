# Details that make the day easier

The template covers information, RSVP, seating, gifts, media and email. A coordinator should also confirm these details before sending invitations:

- Exact addresses, map pins, entrances and local time zones for every event.
- Which households are invited to each event; named plus-ones, children and infants, rather than unlimited guest additions.
- RSVP deadline, meal choices, allergy handling and who follows up on missing replies.
- Step-free access, accessible toilets, seating assistance, quiet space, interpreters and venue contacts.
- Hotel information, parking, shuttle pickup/drop-off times and travel options after the reception.
- Dress code, weather backup, arrival time, ceremony etiquette and an unplugged-photo policy if desired.
- Children, childcare, pets, cultural traditions and any parts of the day requiring additional explanation.
- A day-of contact who is not one of the couple, with an alternate contact and transport disruption plan.
- Seating release timing, table capacities and a printed/offline copy for the venue if connectivity fails.
- Registry wording, applicable currencies and who responds to gift questions. Use a trusted external payment provider.
- Consent for shared photos, upload/storage limits, how long the album remains available, and how guests request removal.
- A final reminder in each language, a post-event thank-you, and a plan to archive/delete personal guest data.

Avoid putting medical details or accessibility requests in public FAQs or ordinary confirmation emails. Collect only what the hosts and venue need, and share those records privately with the people arranging the day.
